★

Three Headlines

FINDING 01 — VOLUME

Complaints concentrate hard. Illegal Parking (~742K), Residential Noise (~560K) and Heat/Hot Water (~492K) alone account for over a third of all requests — and Brooklyn out-complains every other borough.

FINDING 02 — PREDICTABILITY

Resolution time is learnable, to a point. Gradient boosting lifts accuracy from a 59% majority baseline to ~66% and finds the month-long cases the baseline can't see — but 3–30 day cases still blur together.

FINDING 03 — EQUITY

Burden isn't evenly carried. Lower-income neighborhoods log systematically more complaints per resident, which is why the fairness layer adjusts comparisons for demographics before judging disparities.
